About the team

The Avengers are a team of superheroes originally formed by S.H.I.E.L.D. to protect Earth from major threats. The initial team, assembled in response to Loki's invasion, consisted of Iron Man, Captain America, Hulk, Thor, Black Widow, and Hawkeye. The team has since expanded its roster and faced various challenges, becoming an independent organization after S.H.I.E.L.D.'s collapse
